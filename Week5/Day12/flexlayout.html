Charminar

  • Iconic monument built in 1591 in old Hyderabad.
  • Famous for its four grand arches and minarets.
  • Surrounded by lively markets and local food streets.

Golconda Fort

  • Historic fort known for smart acoustic design.
  • Offers panoramic city views from the top.
  • Popular evening sound and light show attraction.

Hussain Sagar Lake

  • Large heart-shaped lake connecting twin cities.
  • Features the giant Buddha statue on Gibraltar Rock.
  • Great spot for boating and sunset views.

Ramoji Film City

  • One of the largest integrated film studio complexes.
  • Known for themed sets, gardens, and live shows.
  • Ideal for family day trips and entertainment.
